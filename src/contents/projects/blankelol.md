blanke.lol is Benjamin's personal site — a single place that brings together his projects, socials, and a short introduction to who he is, without the clutter of a traditional personal website.

Rather than spreading links across bios, profiles, and different platforms, blanke.lol acts as a central hub. Anyone landing on the page gets a quick, clear overview of what Benjamin is working on and where to find him next — whether that's a current project, a social profile, or a way to get in touch.

The site was built with simplicity in mind. Instead of an over-designed personal website trying to do too much, blanke.lol focuses on being fast, clean, and easy to navigate, putting the actual content — the projects and the links that matter — front and center.

At its core, blanke.lol is about **one link, everything you need** — a straightforward home base rather than a sprawling personal site.

[Website](https://blanke.lol)

## What blanke.lol Provides

- **A single entry point** — All of Benjamin's relevant links and projects in one place, instead of scattered across platforms.
- **Fast and lightweight** — Built to load quickly and get out of the way, rather than overwhelming visitors.
- **Clear overview** — A quick way for anyone to see current projects and how to get in touch.
- **Simple design** — No unnecessary complexity — just the essentials, presented cleanly.

## Why blanke.lol?

Personal websites often try to be everything at once — a blog, a portfolio, a resume, and a link hub — which can make them slow to build and harder to keep up to date. blanke.lol takes a more focused approach, acting primarily as a link-in-bio and light portfolio rather than a full personal website.

It's meant to be the kind of page that's quick to update, quick to load, and genuinely useful as a starting point for anyone wanting to learn more or find a specific project.

## Stack

- [Next.js](https://nextjs.org) — A React framework for building fast, modern web applications. Next.js powers blanke.lol's frontend, keeping the site quick and easy to maintain.
