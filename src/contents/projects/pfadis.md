The Pfadfinder Lahr website is the official online home for the scout group, built to give members, parents, and prospective new scouts a clear and welcoming place to learn about what the group does.

Built on WordPress, the site focuses on being easy to maintain and update for a group run largely by volunteers. Rather than requiring technical know-how to keep information current, the platform lets group leaders update news, events, and announcements without needing to touch any code.

The site was created to make Pfadfinder Lahr more approachable to people outside the group — presenting information about meetings, activities, and how to get involved in a straightforward, accessible way. For existing members and parents, it also serves as a central point for announcements and updates.

Ease of maintenance was a key consideration throughout. A scout group's website needs to be something volunteers can realistically keep up to date over time, not a project that requires ongoing technical support just to add a new post or event.

At its core, the Pfadfinder Lahr website is about **an easy-to-maintain home online** — a simple, welcoming presence for a community built around volunteering and the outdoors.

[Website](https://pfadfinder-lahr.de)

## What the Website Provides

- **An accessible introduction** — Clear information about the group for anyone curious about joining.
- **Easy content updates** — Group leaders can post news and updates without technical knowledge.
- **A central point of contact** — Information on meetings, events, and how to get in touch with the group.
- **A welcoming presence** — A simple, approachable site rather than something overly complex.

## Why WordPress?

For a volunteer-run scout group, sustainability matters more than flashy features. WordPress makes it possible for non-technical volunteers to keep the site updated long after the initial build, without relying on a developer for every small change.

## Stack

- [WordPress](https://wordpress.org) — A widely-used content management system that makes it easy for non-technical users to manage and update website content. WordPress powers the Pfadfinder Lahr site, keeping it simple to maintain for the group's volunteers.
